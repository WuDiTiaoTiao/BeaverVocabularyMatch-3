# Vocabulary data

Generated from [ECDICT](https://github.com/skywind3000/ECDICT), licensed under the MIT License.
Synonym pairs use same-synset words from [Princeton WordNet](https://wordnet.princeton.edu/), distributed under the WordNet License.
Complete books include each exam tag plus prerequisite exam vocabulary, so foundational words are not omitted.
High-frequency subsets are ranked from the ECDICT FRQ/BNC, Collins, and Oxford fields.

## Shared meanings

`shared-meanings.json` links each unordered word pair to one specific Princeton WordNet 3.0 synset and a Chinese explanation of that sense. It supplements the original ECDICT meanings; it does not replace or remove them.

Chinese labels use [Chinese Open WordNet](https://github.com/omwn/omw-data/tree/main/wns/cow), Copyright 2013, 2014 Francis Bond and Shan Wang; see [COW-LICENSE.txt](COW-LICENSE.txt).
Additional labels use [Wiktionary via Open Multilingual Wordnet](https://github.com/omwn/omw-data/blob/main/wns/wikt/wn-wikt-cmn.tab), with additional Chinese translations and context labels.
